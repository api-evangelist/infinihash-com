Infinihash LLC builds compliance and infrastructure software for digital assets, offering institutional‑grade KYC, KYT, settlement rails, GPU compute, and private LTE networks. Their platform serves exchanges, OTC desks, DeFi protocols, and regulated financial institutions, providing identity verification, transaction monitoring, on‑chain screening, and customizable settlement solutions. Products are offered as separate services, allowing customers to use compliance tools without the full suite or integrate settlement infrastructure as needed. The company also provides private cellular coverage and GPU compute resources for high‑performance workloads.

**Areas:** Company · FinTech · Crypto · Compliance · KYC · KYT · Infrastructure

## How this profile is made
